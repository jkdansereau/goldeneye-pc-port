## D955: object motion at 90+ fps — props, doors and guards moved at the 60 Hz tick inside a 90 Hz picture — IMPLEMENTED 2026-10-13 (port layer; maintainer Deck check owed)

**Symptom (maintainer, Deck, 90 fps, D954 build).** "Models/objects kinda lag behind in a noticeable way, almost like tearing except nothing is tearing, those just seem to update not at the right speed." Rooms and the camera are smooth.

**Cause (code).** In-between presents draw every modelview exactly as loaded (D578 Cause 2 fix, Deck A/B 2026-10-09: nearest-transform pairing matched identical models to the wrong instance and ghosted); D954 moves only the camera. So every object kept its tick pose for the whole tick: object motion advanced on 2 of 3 presents at 90 Hz.

**Facts the design rests on (verified in code; subagent review `docs/dev/notes/REVIEW-D955-INTERP-OBJECT-MOTION.md`).** GE models are matrix-skinned, not CPU-skinned: each instance's joint matrices for the tick are in `model->render_pos` (dyn pool; `model.c:2423` `render_pos = mtxlist`), converted in place to fixed point, and the model DLs load them through segment 3 (`model.c:5490` `gSPSegment(3, render_pos)`); the vertices are the model file's shared static arrays. The camera is baked into those matrices (`chr.c:2730` `basemtx = camGetWorldToScreenMtxf()`). Every view matrix is relative to `current_model_pos`, the current ROOM's origin (`bondview2.c:8287`), which jumps at a room change. (GEVR's interpolation notes say chrs are CPU-skinned and that the origin changes every frame; both are wrong.)

**Fix (`port/fast3d/gfx_pc.cpp`, `port/src/interpportal.c`; no game code, no game hooks).** Game thread, with the frame's task (D31 snapshot): the on-screen props (`g_OnScreenPropList`) -> per model instance `{render_pos, numMatrices, Model*, model->obj, group, pos}` plus `current_model_pos`; a chr groups with its held weapons, hat and `prop->child` attachments. Explosions, smoke, the viewer and CPU-resized doors (CLIP_TO_BBOX / eye / iris, the `propobj.c:9555` test) are left out. Renderer, once per game frame: read the tick's joint matrices from the instance blocks, pair with the previous frame by (Model*, model->obj), rebase the previous pose onto the current camera, `D = L_{N-1}^-1 * L_N` with `L = R * P^-1` (so the camera is not blended twice: D954 carries it), gate per joint (step <= `GE_INTERPOBJ_STEP` 120 units, rotation < ~35 deg, scale), and decide per group. In the D954 window of a blend pass, a MODELVIEW LOAD whose source lies in an instance block is replaced by `lerp_rows(MV_{N-1} * D, MV_N, alpha)` (basis lengths kept). Exact for: a room-origin change, a non-consecutive or missing previous frame, a changed model file, a failed gate anywhere in the group, split-screen, menus, 60 fps. A previous pose is kept only for instances this frame's DL actually loaded (an on-screen-list entry that was not drawn has a stale `render_pos`). `GE_INTERPOBJ_OFF=1` disables the blend; the 10 s line `D955 obj: ...` reports frames, instances, groups blended/exact, joint loads blended/keyed, unkeyed loads in the window, gate rejects, max step and a static residual.

**Measured (Windows, 1280x800, FpsCap 90, `GE_INTERP_FORCE=1`, scripted turn/walk/strafe).** Rebase check: props whose `pos` did not change land on themselves after `MV_{N-1} * D` to 1.6e-06 mean / 3.1e-05 max (Surface intro flyby, 13,325 joints). On Dam the same residual reads 0.01-0.18 from two single-joint OBJ models whose residual tracks the turn rate (camera-facing sprites; harmless to blend). Dam gameplay, blend on, per 10 s: 33,817 of 33,952 keyed joint loads blended, groups 2,487 blended / 16 exact, 3 gate rejects (a 127-unit step), 2 room-origin changes caught; ~18k unkeyed loads in the window are the room modelviews (~30 rooms x 617 passes); presents 89.6/s (no cost visible on Windows). Surface's spawn has almost nothing on screen and its intro flyby has every present exact (fast camera), so Dam is the test level.

**Owed.** Maintainer Deck check at 90 fps: guards walking across the view (standing and turning), held gun stays in the hand, hats, a guard dying, shadows, doors opening, the Facility bathroom stall doors and sinks while strafing (the D578 ghost site), crossing a room boundary with a guard in view. Vehicles (tank, train) and a per-type gate split are a later stage.

**Pass 2 (2026-10-13, same evening).** (a) The sky and sky water are pre-projected on the CPU for camera N (`sky.c` G_FLOATVTX_EXT clip-space verts), so on in-between presents the horizon stayed at camera N while the world took the blended camera. Per blend pass the camera-space delta `C = P * R^-1 * Rb * P^-1` (rotation part; the sky is at infinity) is applied to each sky vertex: view point rebuilt from (x_c/sx, y_c/sy, -w), rotated, re-projected; S/T unchanged. Dam: 556-589 passes re-projected per 10 s. (b) Destroyed objects draw world-space matrices under the room projection (`propobj.c` objRender `destroyed` branch), where the camera rebase is wrong: object joints are blended only while the props' corrected plain perspective is loaded (`s_pcam_applied`). (c) The first-person weapon is NOT blended: its loads barely ever come from `hand->weaponModel.render_pos` (counted: under 5 per frame), so it is drawn through another matrix path; follow-up. Dam after (a)+(b): 32,013 of 32,140 keyed joint loads blended, 0 gate rejects, 90.1 presents/s.
