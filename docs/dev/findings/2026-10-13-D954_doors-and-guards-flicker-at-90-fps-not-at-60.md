## D954: doors and guards flicker at 90 fps (not at 60) — in-between presents drew props at the exact camera, rooms at the blended one — FIXED 2026-10-13 (port layer; maintainer Deck check owed)

**Symptom (maintainer, Deck, Surface).** Doors and guards flicker at 90 fps; never at 60 fps. First seen on a D953 test build, but nothing in D953 depends on frame rate (LOD is chosen once per game tick and the in-between passes replay the same list), so the trigger is the D578 interpolation, which only runs above 60 fps.

**Cause (code).** GE draws rooms under `field_10E0` (perspective * lookat, `bondview2.c`; room modelviews are scale*translation) and everything else in the world — chrs, doors and other props (`bg.c` `chrpropsRenderPass`), glass, explosions, casings — under the plain perspective `projmatrix`, with the camera in the modelview (`model.c` uses `-mtx->m[3][2]` as view depth). Since the D578 modelview-pairing fix (Deck A/B 2026-10-09) in-between passes blend only the projection and draw modelviews exactly as loaded. So on an in-between present the rooms are drawn at the blended camera and every prop at camera N: whenever the camera moves, props jump against the world, then snap back on the exact present. The older `s_interp_corr` camera correction for modelviews became unreachable with that change (it sits after the modelview early return). Not caused by D953 (which only moves LOD switch distances, identically at 60 and 90 fps) and present in v0.6.0 at 90 fps.

**Measured (Windows, Surface 1, 1280x800, FpsCap 90, `GE_INTERP_FORCE=1`, scripted look left/right).** Per 10 s while turning: 634-637 in-between passes with the props off the room camera, mean 0.39-0.52 deg, max 1.53-1.84 deg (about 5 px mean and up to 22 px at 800 px tall and 60 deg vertical FOV). Not visible at 60 fps (no in-between passes).

**Fix (`port/fast3d/gfx_pc.cpp`, `port/src/interpportal.c`; no game code).** In the world part of a blend pass, a projection LOAD equal to the frame's plain perspective P is replaced by `P * R^-1 * Rb` (R = the frame's room projection, Rb = its blended version from the same `gfx_interp_matrix` pairing and memo the room loads use). Row vectors, `R = L * P`, so `P * R^-1 = L^-1` and a prop vertex lands exactly where a room vertex at the same world point lands. P and R are copied on the game thread with the frame's task (`interpPortalOnSend` snapshot, `cam_ok` only for one player). Window: armed at `gSPSegment(SPSEGMENT_BG_DL)` (`bgLevelRender`: after the sky, before the first room's props), disarmed at the next `gSPPerspNormalize`, which is the first-person gun (`gunfire.c`): the gun inherits the loaded projection and must stay at the exact camera, so the uncorrected P is restored there. Exact passes, split-screen, menus and 60 fps are untouched. `GE_INTERPPROPCAM_OFF=1` disables it. Runtime check, logged once: `R * P^-1` must be affine (measured last column -1.4e-06 0 1.1e-06 1: ok); a failure disables the fix.

**Verified.** Windows build; Surface run as above: convention check ok, 0 passes without a snapshot, the gun disarm hit 431 times per 10 s of gameplay (the remaining passes had no gun drawn). New 10 s log line `D954 prop camera: ...`. Deck build (release recipe: RelWithDebInfo, gcc-14, rpath `$ORIGIN`, bundled SDL2 2.30.0) deployed to the maintainer's shortcut. **Owed:** maintainer Deck check at 90 fps (Surface doors and guards; also the first-person gun while turning, which must not move).

**Status.** FIXED. Maintainer Deck check 2026-10-13: door/guard flicker gone at 90 fps.
