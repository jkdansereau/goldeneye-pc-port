## D956: Switch aim options (zoom, assist, entry, stop on aim) — the guards' hit lists exist only between propsTick and the render passes; one PORT hook in lvlRender — IMPLEMENTED 2026-11-13 (Switch only; checked in Ryujinx and on the console during tuning, final console check owed)

**Request (maintainer, handheld).** Placing a headshot on the 6.2" panel is too hard. Wanted a console-shooter aim: a zoom while aiming, a slowdown of the aim stick on a target, an aim that starts where the auto-aim had put the crosshair, and a character that stops when the aim starts.

**What was built** (`port/src/switch/nxgame.c`, Switch only; rows on the Controller page, grouped between two separators with Aim style and Aim sensitivity, and hidden while Aim style is Original: `Game.AimZoom` off / 1.5x / 2x, 2x by default; `Game.AimAssist` off / low / high, high by default; `Game.AimEntry` and `Game.AimStop`, notes 5 and 6 below).
- *Aim zoom.* While aiming (handheld, one player, centred aim style) `portScaleFovY` divides the rendered fovy, and its floor, by an eased factor. The game's own fovy is untouched, so shots and crosshair are unchanged. The aim stick is divided by the same factor (`nxAimStick`, called from the Switch block of `input.c`), so the camera crosses the screen as fast as without the zoom. With the game's own FOV under 30 (sniper, camera) the Aim sensitivity slider is not applied, the zoom still is.
- *Look precision curve is radial.* `padLookCurveApply` ran per axis: a full diagonal (0.7, 0.7) came out at 0.35 per axis, about half the speed of a full horizontal push. `padLookCurveRadial` applies the curve to the stick's magnitude and keeps its direction.
- *Aim assist.* Once per frame the ray through the screen centre is tested against the on-screen armed guards, with four side rays a few screen units away for half strength. Strength eases in fast (0.03 s) and out slowly (0.15 s); 0.7x (low) or 0.5x (high) on a guard. The test is the game's `chrTestHit` (a bullet's, so body, head and arms). A coarser variant on the guard's bounding sphere (`projectileTestPropBoundingSphere`, no hit list needed) was tried and dropped.

**Facts the design rests on (verified in code; Dam in Ryujinx with an on-screen readout, 2026-11-13).**
1. *The game's own auto-aim target is not usable.* `chrpropUpdateAutoaimTarget` runs every frame, aiming or not, but `chrpropScoreAutoAimTarget` only accepts a guard within `1.5 x its screen width x difficulty` of the centre, and the width comes from `modelGetAxisExtents` (BBOX nodes), which comes back empty on the PC build (projected left == right == screen centre). On Agent 00 it only fires point blank, and its aim point is the torso. Not used.
2. *Lifetime of `chr->field_20` (the `ModelHitEntry` list `chrTestHit` walks).* Built in the chr tick (`chr.c` 2756, under `propsTick`, `lv.c` 836); freed at the next tick (`chr.c` 2682) and by the alpha render pass (`chr.c` 3225). `chrTestHit` with a NULL list dereferences NULL in `sub_GAME_7F06C010` (`objecthandler.c` 1272, fault address 0x18): that was the crash when the test ran on the display-list thread (`optionsOverlayEmit`, `portRenderWorker`). Hook points tried, hit lists present on the guards: start of the frame / `bondview2.c` before `gunTickGameplay` (none), `lv.c` after `bgLevelRender` (none), `lv.c` right before `chraiCheckUseHeldItems` (all). The last is where the game tests the player's own shot (`lv.c` 839), so the list, the on-screen flag, the matrices and `g_OnScreenPropList` are the ones a bullet sees.
3. *Side effects of probing.* `bullet_path_from_screen_center` draws `RANDOMFRAC` (it would desync the game's PRNG), so the ray direction comes from the pure `transformAndNormalizeByLength2Dto3D`. `chrTestHit` flags `CHRFLAG_NEAR_MISS` and bumps `numclosearghs` (a guard flinches); both are restored after each probe.
4. *Line of sight.* The game's `stanTestLineUnobstructed` + "ends in the guard's tile" check (`chrpropScoreAutoAimTarget`) alternated yes/no from frame to frame on a guard in the open; a refusal is therefore ignored until it holds 8 frames in a row. Why it flickers is not established (the readout never got a frame on target with the split counters).

5. *The crosshair leaving the guard at aim entry.* Off the aim the game's hip-fire auto-aim (`bondview2.c` 6214-6242, `canAutoAim = !insightaimmode`) pulls `crosshair_x_pos/_y_pos` onto the guard. In Centered aim the port feeds no stick to the game's crosshair integrator, so the game's own damping relaxes the crosshair to the centre, slower than a few frames, and the guard slides away from it. `Game.AimEntry` (`nxAimEntryStep`, called from the pad's Centered branch in `input.c`) has three settings: Off; "Move view", a scripted slide of 0.08 s in which the camera turns by the angle between the crosshair and the centre while `crosshair_x/y_pos` walk to the centre in step (screen offset = tan(angle) x `c_recipscale`, back to a position through the units-per-position measured while off the aim), so the crosshair stays on the same point of the world and both are in place after about five frames; "Centre crosshair", which zeroes the positions at once. The start is the crosshair as last seen OFF the aim, not as first seen in it: the game has already run its first aim tick, and begun relaxing the crosshair, when `insightaimmode` is seen. History (console, 2026-11-13): following the game's own relaxation moved the view only part of the way (a 0.6 s cap, then the lost first tick), each new press of ZL added a little more, and the slide was too long to feel like an instant aim. Signs: `vv_theta` grows turning right; `vv_verta` RISES looking up (the first guess, the opposite, pulled the view away from the guard in Ryujinx, Dam, Agent).

6. *The character gliding after the stick is released* (about 1 s and 110+ game units, measured on the console with an on-screen readout of the walk speeds and of the distance walked after the release; identical with the option off and on, so not the speeds). The walk speeds are ramps that fall to 0 in one tick (`g_GlobalTimerDelta` = `g_ClockTimer`, an integer). What pushes Bond afterwards is `headpos`: `bondviewMoveAnimationTick` runs the walk animation, `bheadUpdate` feeds its per-tick velocity into `bheadUpdatePos` (`bondhead.c`), a single-pole filter (`headpossum = 0.93 x headpossum + velocity`, `headpos = headpossum x 0.07`), and `bondview2.c` adds `headpos` x `g_GlobalTimerDelta` to `move_offset` every tick. Released, the input is 0 but the filter keeps its charge and goes on pushing Bond. `Game.AimStop` ("Stop on aim", on by default; `nxAimStopStep`, from the pad's Centered branch in `input.c`) empties `headpossum` and `headpos` on x and z (the height, the head bob, is kept) and zeroes the speeds while the aim is on and the walk stick is at rest (an "always" setting that also stopped him whenever the stick was released was tried and judged unpleasant); any walk input hands control back to the game, and a cutscene (`gameScriptedCameraActive`) or a vehicle (`g_PlayerIsInTank`) is left alone. The first versions zeroed only the speeds (and only while aiming) and changed nothing the maintainer could feel. Console check owed.

**Game-file edit (rule 2; maintainer asked for the exact-hit-test option, 2026-11-13).** `src/game/lv.c`: one block under `#if defined(PORT) && defined(__SWITCH__)` that calls `nxAimAssistFrame()` before `chraiCheckUseHeldItems()`. No game logic changes (the probes restore what they touch). No other game file is edited for this.

**Also in this change.** The console's Aim style has two settings, Original and Centered: the weapon sway (`padAimSway`) is part of Centered, the former "Centered + sway" (`Input.AimMode` 2) is gone and the setting is registered 0..1. The Switch controller defaults are the maintainer's tuned values (both stick deadzones 10 %, look sensitivity 0.85x, vertical look inverted, look smoothing 2, trigger threshold 10 %, Aim sensitivity 0.75x, Look precision 2.0, Turn acceleration high; Reset to defaults follows). The Switch rows added in this fork have descriptions that fit two lines at HUD 150 (checked with the log's tip check; a few older rows, such as the display mode, still overflow). The options entry reads "Options", not "PC Options".

**Owed.** Console check, handheld, of the final build: zoom 2x / 1.5x, Aim sensitivity, assist low / high, a guard behind a wall or glass (the line-of-sight hold), Aim entry, Stop on aim. Solo only (split-screen is left alone on purpose).
