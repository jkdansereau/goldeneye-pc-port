## D957: Look ahead never fires with the Extended controls on the Switch — the game's condition needs an analogue walk the scheme never produces — IMPLEMENTED 2026-11-13 (Switch only; tuned in Ryujinx, console check owed)

**Symptom (maintainer, handheld).** Walking while looking at the floor; the per-file "Look ahead" option does nothing.

**Cause (code).** The game's look ahead (`bondview2.c` 5950-5995, behind `automovecentreenabled`) starts only when `moveData.canLookAhead` and `moveData.analogWalk > 60` (or < -60), with no manual pitch input, and the pitch more than 5 degrees above or 10 below the target (-4, or the slope ahead). In the Extended scheme (`input.c`, the SOLITARE-role branch) the left stick is sent as the digital steps (C buttons), so `analogWalk` is 0 and the condition is never true. It would also be locked after any manual pitch until the walk stick returned under 40 (`movecentrerelease`), so it recentres when you START walking, not while you walk looking down.

**Fix (`port/src/switch/nxgame.c` `nxLookAheadStep`, one call in the pad's Centered branch of `input.c`; no game code).** A separate option, `Game.LookAhead` ("Look ahead (Extended)", Controller page; off / low 45 / medium 60 / high 90 degrees per second at most, high by default; hidden while Aim style is Original); the game's own per-file Look ahead stays on the Game page, untouched. Walking forward with the left stick, the look stick at rest for 0.5 s, not aiming, Centered aim style, the pitch eases back to 0 (level) once it is more than 5 degrees above or 10 below (the game's thresholds), constant speed by setting over the whole way, slowing over the last 6 degrees (the first version, an exponential ease with a time constant of 0.22 s capped by the speed, made low / medium / high feel alike: only its first moments depended on the speed). Tuned by the maintainer in Ryujinx: the first idle delay (0.35 s) was lengthened to 0.5 s, which is right; 130 degrees per second came up too fast; 15 and 45 were tried, then the maintainer asked for three speeds plus off and settled on 45 / 60 / 90; and the game's own base pitch of -4 (a view a little low, `bondview2.c` 5890) was rejected as the target, level is the usual one. Flat horizon only; the game's slope following is not reproduced.

**Owed.** Console check: walk forward after looking down or up; the view should level out after a moment and stop when the look stick is touched. Making the game's own look ahead work would need an analogue walk, i.e. the game's control definition changed (and styles 1.2 and 1.4 set `moveData.disableLookAhead` anyway, `bondview2.c` 5519): a separate project.
