## D961: Switch controls feel laggy when the render thread is slow -- the scheduler starts a submitted frame only at the next retrace; GE_LOWLAT / GE_PRESSLATCH / INPUTLAT added -- BUILT 2026-11-14 (Switch only; port layer only; NOTHING RUN, console A/B owed)

**Symptom.** Real console, 2-player co-op: render thread's display-list pass 14-21 ms, 39-49 presents/s, game thread 0.7-0.9 ms per message, GPU 4-6 ms; controls feel late and short presses are sometimes missed, also with `Video.FpsCap=30`.

**(1) Path of one press (read from the code, nothing measured).** `SDL_GameControllerUpdate` in `inputUpdate` (`port/src/input.c:1889`) runs on the SCHEDULER thread from `contSnapshotFromKeyboard` (`port/src/libultra.c:1308`), called by the `osContStartReadData` shim (`:1346`) inside `joyPoll()` (`src/sched.c:308/321`, once per retrace tick, 60 Hz, independent of the render thread). `joyPoll` collects a read one poll AFTER starting it (N64 SI semantics, `src/joy.c:463-500`): `osContGetReadData` (`libultra.c:1372`) copies the snapshot taken at the PREVIOUS tick, so every sample is already 16.7 ms old when it enters the ring (`g_ContData[0].samples`, 20 entries). The render thread also runs `SDL_JoystickUpdate` through its `SDL_PollEvent` (`gfx_sdl2.cpp` `gfx_sdl_handle_events`, D959) at its own instants, but nobody samples the pad there. The game consumes the ring only when it builds a frame: retrace message on `gfxFrameMsgQ` -> `boss.c:625` tick gate -> `waitForNextFrame` -> `joyConsumeSamplesWrapper` (`boss.c:646`, rising edges OR-ed over all samples since the last build; held state = newest sample) -> logic -> `lvlRender` -> `rspGfxTaskStart` -> `osSendMesg(cmdQ)`. Sampling is therefore at the START of the build, about 1 ms before the submit. The FpsCap limiter (`gfx_sdl2.cpp:685`, `sync_framerate_with_timer` before the swap) runs on the render thread after the display-list pass and neither before nor after any sampling: it only lengthens the render time and so the wait of the next frame.

**(2) Hand-off game -> render.** The game builds when `pendingGfx < 2` (`boss.c:632`): at most one frame rendering plus one waiting (depth 2). The D481 comment "slot always empty" is true for the worker mailbox only: the waiting frame is not in the mailbox, it is in `sched_cmdQ` / `sc->gfxListHead`. KEY FINDING: `src/sched.c` moves a task from `cmdQ` to the RCP only inside `__scHandleRetrace` (`sched.c:324`, `__scAppendList`), i.e. at the NEXT 16.7 ms tick; `__scHandleRSP` only schedules what is already in the lists. A frame the game submits 1-2 ms after a tick therefore waits about 15 ms before the render worker even sees it, and a render that ends between two ticks leaves the RCP idle until the next tick if nothing was appended earlier. Hand-simulated with R=20 ms (build at the first tick after the previous frame started, append one tick later): render starts are about 30 ms apart (33 fps) although R alone allows 50; the measured 39-49 fps fits that quantisation better than R alone. The queue does fill when the renderer is the bottleneck: each displayed frame is built one tick after the previous one started, appended one tick later, and starts when the previous finishes. The limiter (cap 30) makes R = 33 ms and the same frame waits 33 ms more.

**(3) Expected latency (model, NOT measured; INPUTLAT gives the real numbers).** age = sample -> swap return. Sample age at build about 17.5 ms (one poll behind) + build 1 ms + wait for the next tick about 15 ms + wait for the previous frame (0 .. R) + R + swap. At 60 presents/s (D600 phase lock, queue empty): about 17.5 + 1 + 15 + 8 + 7 (vblank wait) = 50 ms. At 40-50 presents/s (R 14-21): about 60-70 ms. At a 30 fps cap (R = 33): about 85-95 ms. The dominant parts are the two one-tick delays (sample one poll old, frame waits for the next tick), then the queue behind the rendering frame.

**(4) Lost presses.** Rising edges between two builds are NOT lost at the joy level (`joyConsumeSamples` ORs `buttons & ~prev` over every ring sample; the ring only overflows after 19 unconsumed polls, `joy.c:471`). Two real losses: (a) the pad is sampled by value at 60 Hz (plus the render thread's `SDL_JoystickUpdate` instants that nobody reads), so a press shorter than 16.7 ms that falls between two samples is invisible; (b) held-state readers (fire, stick-like uses) see only the NEWEST sample at build, and builds are 25-33 ms apart when the renderer is slow, so a 17-33 ms tap that sits between two builds' newest samples is released by the time of the next build. (b) is the realistic "short press missed" case; the N64 had it too at 20-30 fps, the port adds the one-poll-old sample and the queue.

**Changes (port code only, no `src/` edit; both switches read once in `portLatInit`, `libultra.c:310`; unset = no behaviour change, only counters).**
- Measurement (`GE_PERFSTAT=1`): `portLatNoteSubmit` (`libultra.c:1633`, game thread, hooked in `osSendMesg:846` on the `sched_cmdQ` / `g_gfxTaskSettingsList` message, same key as `interpPortalOnSend`) stamps each submitted frame with the delivered pad-sample time and the submit time; `portLatBegin` (render pickup) and `portLatEnd` (`:1718`, swap returned) close it, in the worker and in the inline path. Every 300 frames: `INPUTLAT samples=N age_avg=..ms age_max=..ms queue_avg=.. taps_saved=.. sub2start_avg=..ms render_avg=..ms kicks=.. gate_skips=.. lowlat=0|1 latch=0|2..4`. age = swap return minus the stamp of the newest sample in the ring at submit; queue_avg = frames in flight (rendering or waiting) at submit, 0 = idle renderer, 1 = one ahead; sub2start = submit -> render pickup (the proof of the retrace quantisation: about 15 ms without the kick); render = pickup -> swap return. Not meaningful with D578 interpolation (age ends at the last drawn pass).
- `GE_LOWLAT=1`: (a) fresh sample: `osContStartReadData` no longer snapshots, `osContGetReadData` does (`libultra.c:1346/1372`), removing the one-poll lag; (b) scheduler kick: `osSendMesg` of a gfx task wakes the scheduler thread (`portSchedKickRequest:1683`), `osRecvMesg` serves it on that thread between two handlers (`portSchedKick:1693`): drain `cmdQ` + `__scSchedule` + `__scExec`; nothing else of `__scHandleRetrace` runs (no joyPoll, music fade, frame count, client messages), so tick-driven timing is untouched; skipped for GE_DETERM, inline render, D578 interpolation, and while an audio task needs the yield; (c) build gate `portLowLatSkipTick:1655`: with exactly one frame rendering and more than one tick + build time + 1.5 ms left (EMA of the render time), the retrace message is dropped inside `osRecvMesg` (the game would only queue a frame behind the render), at most 3 in a row. The gate rarely fires for R < 33 ms; (a) and the kick carry the effect.
- `GE_PRESSLATCH=1` (or n = 2..4): (a) min-hold `portPressHold:346` on the N64 button mask: a press seen for fewer than n consecutive polls (default 2 = one 30 fps frame) is shown for n polls; one run per press, no second edge, a second press within the hold merges (about 33 ms); stick axes untouched. (b) event latch `input.c:733-775`: `SDL_CONTROLLERBUTTONDOWN` and trigger-axis events seen by the render thread's pump (`gfx_sdl2.cpp:510`) are OR-ed into the next gameplay sample once (`padLatchApply`, applied at the single gameplay raw read, `input.c:3246`), with a guard that drops a DOWN the previous sample already showed held (no double edge). taps_saved = min-holds + latched taps.

**Console A/B (maintainer; one nxlink client at a time).** Same level, 40 s each, args `-level_37 -hard0 GE_COOP=1 GE_PERFSTAT=1 NX_TEST_SECS=40`: run A baseline; run B + `GE_LOWLAT=1`; run C + `GE_LOWLAT=1 GE_PRESSLATCH=1`; repeat A and B at `Video.FpsCap=30`, and a solo level (e.g. Dam) to check nothing regresses at 60. Decide on the `INPUTLAT` lines (second and later: the first window includes the load): `age_avg` should fall by about 15-30 ms with LOWLAT, `sub2start_avg` should drop from about 15 to under 2 ms with `kicks` about one per frame; the `PERFSTAT presents=` lines must not fall (the kick should raise 39-49 toward 1/R); `queue_avg` shows the depth. Check in play that music fades, rumble and sound are not off-tempo (no extra ticks are generated). If `kicks=0` with `lowlat=1`, the render worker was not running or interpolation is on. Feel test: tap fire and A quickly in co-op with and without PRESSLATCH.

**Confidence.** (1) high (read, every hop cited). (2) high for the queue depth and for "cmdQ is drained only at retrace" (read `sched.c`: `__scSchedule` only looks at the lists); medium for the fps effect (hand simulation). (3) low: arithmetic from the model, not a measurement. (4) high that (a) and (b) exist, medium that (b) explains what the maintainer feels. Fix: medium; the kick is the part most likely to change feel and fps, it is untested on any device and runs `__scExec` from the shim on the scheduler thread (the same thread as the original call, so no new race, but the order of SP/DP done versus the next tick changed). Not done: a sample taken right before the build (needs `joyPoll` between ticks, game code) and tick steering to render completions (changes the retrace rate that drives the audio manager at tick/2, `audio.c:297`: audio would be generated slower than played).
