    Play online

    1. Get the GoldenEye 007 PC port and your own legally owned ROM.
    2. In the game, press F9 (or pick Online on the file screen).
    3. Quick match puts you in the next open game that fits Quick match settings (mode, map, weapons, length, players — each can be “any”); Browse games lists the ones above; a friend's 6-letter code joins their game directly.

    Every player runs the game on their own PC and sees only their own view. Games connect player to player — this service only introduces them.

    Names are shown as players typed them; private games appear without names or codes. No accounts, no tracking. Updates live.
